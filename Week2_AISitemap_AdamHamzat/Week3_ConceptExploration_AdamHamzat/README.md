## The three directions
- Concept A, Clean Utility: light, search-first, information-rich
- Concept B, Immersive Dark: dark theme, bold hero, premium feel
- Concept C, Community Local: warm, seller-led, chat-to-buy

## My pick
Concept A, because it lets shoppers search and compare quickly and builds trust with visible prices and ratings. See the note for the full reasoning.
