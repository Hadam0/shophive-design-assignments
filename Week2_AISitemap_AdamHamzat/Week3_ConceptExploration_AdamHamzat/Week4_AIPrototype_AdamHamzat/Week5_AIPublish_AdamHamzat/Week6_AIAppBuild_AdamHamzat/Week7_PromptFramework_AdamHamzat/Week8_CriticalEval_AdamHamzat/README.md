**Name:** Adam Hamzat
**Annotated Figma board:** https://www.figma.com/design/UwaeIAxZWKL9vcxBfsEGLA/FLEXISAF?node-id=2273-302&t=LIqocV9Ht61z9qpI-1
## What I evaluated
The original AI-generated Home screen from Week 2 of the ShopHive marketplace app (generated by Claude in the first design pass).

## Tool used
Claude AI

## The 7 problems and fixes
1. Generic: every product image was the same grey box. Fix: real product photos and a discount badge.
2. Wrong: category icons did not match their categories. Fix: shirt, phone, sparkles, sofa and basket icons.
3. Wrong: the banner copy was generic and contradicted the products. Fix: specific copy that matches what is for sale.
4. Off-brand: the "Shop now" button was orange and only 28px high. Fix: on-brand green and white 44px button.
5. Wrong: the bell (40px) and hearts (32px) were below the 44px tap target. Fix: all set to 44px.
6. Wrong: the search placeholder had a contrast ratio of 4.39:1, below the 4.5:1 AA minimum. Fix: darker text at 6.87:1.
7. Off-brand: product cards had no trust cues or add-to-cart action. Fix: verified-seller mark, rating, original price and a 44px Add to cart button.
