https://shophivemarketplace.netlify.app

## Figma design
(https://www.figma.com/design/UwaeIAxZWKL9vcxBfsEGLA/FLEXISAF?node-id=2155-3341&t=usUA7UjemerPZRwy-1)

## Tool used
Claude (AI assistant) generated the first version of the website from a written description of my project. I did not use Framer AI. I refined the design by hand in Figma.

## What the AI produced
A complete landing page: header, hero with app preview, shop categories, trust features, how it works, popular deals, a Sell on ShopHive section, a waitlist form and a footer. The layout adapts from desktop to mobile.

## What I changed after the AI's first pass
1. Hero background: replaced the flat pale green with a soft diagonal gradient to add depth.
2. Hero circle: added a bold brand-green circle behind the phone to create a stronger focal point.
3. Phone screen: replaced grey placeholder boxes with real product photos.
4. Deals section: replaced grey placeholders on every product card with real product photos.

## Why the first pass was not quite right
The structure and copy were good, so I kept them. But the design looked flat and unfinished, like a template. My changes added depth, brand colour and real imagery.

## What I learned
AI is quick at producing a solid, responsive structure. Making it feel like a real brand takes human decisions about colour, depth and imagery.
